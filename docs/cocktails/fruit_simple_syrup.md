# Fruit Simple Syrup (청)
<img src="https://hardershawaii.com/cdn/shop/products/Mango-25oz_9e1d60bf-1457-4422-8786-1ec810ba4687.jpg" alt="syrup" width="320"/>  

## ℹ️ info
* About 3 days  
* 12 servings  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **200	grams**	fruit
- [ ] **200	grams**	sugar
- [ ] Top-up water *(as needed)*

## 🥃 steps
1. Layer sugar and fruit in a glass jar or bowl.
2. Cover tightly and leave at room temperature, stirring twice each day, until the sugar is completely dissolved, up to 3 days.
3. Strain mixture through fine-mesh strainer into a pint mason jar, pressing on solids to extract as much liquid as possible.
4. Weigh the syrup. If the syrup weighs less than double the weight of sugar used, in the jar used for macerating, combine the fruit solids with an amount of top-up water needed to equal double the weight of sugar used.
5. Stir the mixture, then strain again through fine-mesh strainer into the mason jar, pressing on solids to extract as much liquid as possible; discard solids.

## ✏️ notes
* Works well with mango, lilikoi, berries, citrus, etc.
* Can also be made with only pits and peels by increasing the amount of fruit used to double the amount of sugar by weight.
* Store in the refrigerator for up to 1 month.
* For more accuracy, measure the brix of the syrup after step 3 with a refractometer. Adjust syrup with top-up water to equal 50 brix.
