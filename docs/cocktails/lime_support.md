# Lime Support
<img src="https://www.kroger.com/product/images/xlarge/front/0001660000103" width="320"/>  

## ℹ️ info
* makes 500 ml  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **30	grams**	lime peel
- [ ] **30	grams**	granulated sugar
- [ ] **20	grams**	citric acid
- [ ] **10	grams**	malic acid
- [ ] **1	grams**	kosher salt
- [ ] **500	grams**	water

## 🥃 steps
1. Peel your citrus with minimal pith and weigh.
2. Add the dry ingredients on top of the peel.
3. For best results, sit for 2–6 hours.
4. Add water and blend for 15–30 seconds.
5. Fine strain, bottle and refrigerate.

## ✏️ notes
* Will last in the fridge up to 3 weeks
