# Quick-pickled Red Onions
<img src="https://www.davidlebovitz.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/09/Carnitas.jpg" alt="red_onion" width="320"/>  

## ℹ️ info
* About 10 minutes  
* 1 pint canning jar  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **240	grams**	red wine vinegar
- [ ] **69	grams**	granulated sugar
- [ ] **2	grams**	Diamond Crystal kosher salt *(1/2 tsp)*
- [ ] **240	grams**	red onion, halved and sliced thin through root end *(1 large)*

## 🔪 steps
1. Bring vinegar, sugar, and salt to simmer in small saucepan over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until sugar has dissolved.
2. Off heat, stir in onion, cover, and let cool to room temperature, about 1 hour. Serve.

## ✏️ notes
* Pickled onions can be refrigerated in airtight container for up to 1 week.
