# Italian Meatloaf Sandwiches
<img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/hksqkdlah/image/upload/SFS_ItalianMeatloafSandwich-80_fyid7h" alt="meatloaf_sandwich" width="320"/>  

## ℹ️ info
* About 120 minutes  
* 6-8 servings  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **35	squares**	saltines *(1 sleeve)*
- [ ] **180	grams**	whole milk
- [ ] **100	grams**	eggs *(2 large)*
- [ ] **454	grams**	85% lean ground beef
- [ ] **454	grams**	plain pork sausage, casings removed
- [ ] **57	grams**	Parmesan cheese, grated
- [ ] **4	grams**	granulated garlic *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **1	grams**	dried oregano *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **3	grams**	Diamond Crystal kosher salt *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **1	grams**	ground black pepper *(1/2 tsp)*
- [ ] **1	grams**	red pepper flakes *(1/8 tsp)*
- [ ] **1000	grams**	tomato sauce

## 🔪 steps
1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 400 degrees. Spray 2 quart baking dish with vegetable oil spray. Place saltines in large zipper-lock bag, seal bag, and crush saltines to fine crumbs with rolling pin. Whisk saltines, milk, and eggs together in large bowl. Let sit until saltines are softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Whisk saltine mixture until smooth paste forms. Add beef, sausage, Parmesan, granulated garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, and pepper flakes and mix with your hands until thoroughly combined.
3. Transfer beef mixture to prepared dish. Using your wet hands, shape into 8 by 5-inch rectangle; top should be flat and meatloaf should be 2-1/2 inches thick. Pour sauce over meatloaf. Cover dish with aluminum foil and place on rimmed baking sheet. Bake until meatloaf registers 160 degrees, 1-1/4 hours.
4. Remove sheet from oven, uncover dish, and let rest for 15 minutes.
5. Spoon off any excess grease from tomato sauce. Slice meatloaf 1 inch thick with bench scraper. Place bread bottom on prepared sheet pan. Spread tomato sauce on bread bottom, then top with meatloaf. Spread tomato sauce over meatloaf. Top meatloaf with shredded mozzarella cheese.
6. Broil in toaster oven until cheese is melted, 2 to 3 minutes. Sprinkle with basil. Top with bread top, slice in half, and serve.
