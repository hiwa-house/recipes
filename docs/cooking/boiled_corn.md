# Boiled Corn
<img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/hksqkdlah/image/upload/31790_sfs-boiled-corn-19" alt="corn" width="320"/>  

## ℹ️ info
* About 30 minutes  
* 4-6 servings  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **4	ears**	corn, shucked

## 🔪 steps
1. Bring 4 quarts water to boil in large Dutch oven. Turn off heat, add corn to water, cover, and let stand for at least 10 minutes or up to 30 minutes.
2. Transfer corn to large platter and serve immediately, passing gochujang miso sauce, butter, salt, pepper, or other toppings, as desired.
