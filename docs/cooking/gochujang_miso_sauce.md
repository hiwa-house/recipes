## ℹ️ info
* About 10 minutes  
* 8 servings  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **15	grams**	white miso paste *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] **15	grams**	gochujang *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] **15	grams**	mayonnaise *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] **8	grams**	juice from 1/2 lemon *(1/2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **1/4	cup**	roughly chopped fresh cilantro leaves
- [ ] **1/4	cup**	finely sliced green onions

## 🔪 steps
1. Combine miso, gochujang, mayonnaise, lemon juice, half of cilantro, and half of scallions. Whisk until homogenous. Set aside.
6. Serve with corn or other vegetables, topping with remaining cilantro and scallions.
