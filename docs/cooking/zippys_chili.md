# Zippy's Chili

## ℹ️ info
* Pressure Cooker: about 60 minutes  
* Oven: about 2 hours  
* 6–8 servings  

## 📋 ingredients
- [ ] **454 grams** ground beef, at least 15% fat *(1 lb)*
- [ ] **140 grams** Portuguese sausage, crumbled with a blender *(5 oz)*
- [ ] **1/2 teaspoon** Kosher salt
- [ ] **1/2 teaspoon** baking soda
- [ ] **9 grams** vegetable oil, plus more if needed *(2 tsp)*
- [ ] **100 grams** yellow onion, finely diced *(1/2 medium)*
- [ ] **40 grams** celery, finely diced *(1 stalk)*
- [ ] **1 whole** green bell pepper, finely diced
- [ ] **2 whole** cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] **12 grams** chili powder *(2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **1/4 teaspoon** onion powder
- [ ] **1/4 teaspoon** garlic powder
- [ ] **2	whole****	bay leaf
- [ ] **33 grams** tomato paste *(2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **298 grams** cream of mushroom soup *(1 can)*
- [ ] **425 grams** crushed tomatoes *(1 can)*
- [ ] **8	grams** chicken bouillon *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **180 grams** reserved bean liquid
- [ ] **880 grams** red kidney beans, drained (or 130 g cooked dry beans, see note) *(2 cans)*
- [ ] **4	grams** granulated sugar *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **3	grams** Asian fish sauce *(1/2 tsp)*
- [ ] **10	grams** cider vinegar *(2 tsp)*

## 🔪 steps
1. Whisk *30 grams* water, salt, and baking soda in a medium bowl until dissolved. Using a hand, whisk beef with mixture until thoroughly combined and let it sit while prepping, about 15-20 minutes.
2. Heat oil in medium pressure cooker *(or Dutch oven)* over medium-high heat, until shimmering. Add onion, celery, and bell pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4 to 6 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Add the beef and Portuguese sausage; break into 1/4-inch pieces with a wooden spoon. Cook until browned and a fond forms on the bottom, 12 to 14 minutes.
4. Stir in chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, and bay leaf; cook about 1 minute until fragrant. Add tomato paste and cook, stirring, until it darkens slightly, about 1 minute.
5. Pour in the reserved bean liquid and crushed tomatoes, stirring well and scraping bottom all over. Stir in the cream of mushroom soup and bouillon until fully combined.
6. **Pressure Cooker:** Seal and bring to high pressure. Cook ***15 minutes***, then allow pressure to release naturally and open lid. Or, **Oven:** Transfer the pot to a ***275°F*** oven, covered. Cook ***90 minutes***, stirring once or twice, then remove from oven.
7. Discard bay leaf and stir in the drained beans. Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat, stirring, ***5-8 minutes***, until heated through and thickened to taste.               
8. Add sugar, fish sauce, and cider vinegar, stirring to combine. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
9. Serve over rice with Redondo's Hawaiian Winner.

## ✏️ notes
* To cook dried red kidney beans ***(optional)***: Before making the chili, add 130 grams dried red kidney beans to medium pressure cooker, add 1/2 teaspoon salt, and cover with 3 inches of water. Bring to high pressure, cook for ***22 minutes***, then release pressure naturally. Transfer beans, in their cooking liquid, to container until ready to use.
